Hello! I am a master's student in Computer Science and Technology at the [Machine Intelligence and Translation Lab](https://mitlab.hit.edu.cn/) (Language Technology Research Center), Harbin Institute of Technology, under the supervision of [Prof. Tiejun Zhao](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/zhaotiejun).

My research focuses on Multimodal Large Language Models, Multi-Agent Systems, Multilingual LLMs, and Multimodal Reasoning.

I am interested in open-source software and Linux.
