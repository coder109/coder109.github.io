Hello! I'm a master's student in Computer Science and Technology at **Harbin Institute of Technology**, where I work with the [Machine Intelligence and Translation Lab](https://mitlab.hit.edu.cn/) (Language Technology Research Center), advised by [Prof. Tiejun Zhao](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/zhaotiejun).

My research interests include **Multimodal and Multilingual LLMs, Multi-Agent Systems, and Multimodal Reasoning**. I'm particularly interested in how language models can reason, collaborate, and interact with the world.

Beyond research, I'm an open-source enthusiast and enjoy exploring Linux and the broader software ecosystem.

# 🛠 What I Work With

Most of my work revolves around **LLMs and multi-agent systems**. I primarily use **Python**, along with **PyTorch** and the **Hugging Face ecosystem**, for model training, fine-tuning, and evaluation. I also work with **C/C++** and enjoy exploring algorithms and how things work under the hood.

I'm particularly interested in **LLM post-training**, from supervised fine-tuning and parameter-efficient methods like LoRA to reinforcement learning with reward models and verifiable feedback.

On the agent side, I enjoy exploring how LLMs can **plan, use tools, collaborate, and verify their own work**. I've been working with multi-agent architectures, task decomposition, and tool integration, including MCP and agent skills.

I usually develop in **Linux environments**, manage experiments and code with **Git/GitHub**, and care about reproducibility, thoughtful evaluation, and clean implementations.
